How to play

  • Move: drag your finger left and right on the game. On a PC use the arrow keys or A / D.
  • Catch coins and gems. Dodge bombs. Lives depend on your difficulty setting.
  • The mission is shown at the top of every stage.
  • Brain stages show a puzzle card: make an exact total, follow a rule, repeat a colour order, or answer a maths question.
  • A wrong catch costs a life from stage 20 on Normal.
  • Power-ups: S shield, M magnet, T slow, x2 double, W wide.
  • Open Settings for difficulty, music, vibration, puzzle assist and more.
